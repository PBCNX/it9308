# 048D:9309 - moved to its own repo

The 9309 is a different device from the 9308: an IT9303 bridge with a
**Panasonic MN88553H** tuner, not the Sony demod, and a different register
architecture.  It now lives in `../it9309`:

    ../it9309/docs/9309.md      the full findings
    ../it9309/tools/            win_session.py, mn_configure.py, mn_ini.py
    ../it9309/it9309/tuner.py   the driver seed

What stays here is what the two devices genuinely share: `it9308/transport.py`
(the IT930x bridge protocol), the protocol notes, and the generic vendor-library
triage in `tools/so_diff.py` and `tools/elfinfo.py`.

Short version, for anyone who lands here first: the MN88553H answers on **bus 3
at 7-bit 0x60/0x61/0x62** (wire bytes 0xc0/0xc2/0xc4) once **GPIO2 is driven
high** as an output.  Earlier notes in this repo called the board parked and an
"unreleased IT9308 variant" - it is alive, and only the bridge is shared.
